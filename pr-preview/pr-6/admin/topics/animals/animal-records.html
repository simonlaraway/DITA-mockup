Animal records

An animal record holds everything your shelter knows about one animal: who they are, where they are, their health, and their history with you.

Open any record to see its tabs:

  • Profile: name, breed, age, size, color, microchip, and a public description for adoption listings.
  • Medical: vaccinations, treatments, weight history and spay/neuter status.
  • Behavior: temperament notes, playgroup compatibility and handling alerts.
  • Timeline: every event, newest first, with who recorded it.
  • Documents: intake forms, vet records and signed adoption contracts.

Handling alerts

When an animal needs special handling, for example "two-person handling" or "no small dogs", add a handling alert on the Behavior tab. Alerts appear as a red banner wherever the animal is shown, including on kennel cards and in the CorgiBase Kennel app.

Returning animals

If an animal comes back to your shelter, CorgiBase reopens the existing record instead of creating a new one, so you keep the animal's full history.
