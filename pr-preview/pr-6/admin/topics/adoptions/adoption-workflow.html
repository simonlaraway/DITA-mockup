How adoptions work

Adoptions in CorgiBase move through four stages, from application to going home. Each stage is recorded on both the animal's and the adopter's timeline.

  1. Apply. An adopter applies through your website's listing or at the front desk. Applications appear under Adoptions with status New.
  2. Review. Staff check references and landlord approval, and can request a meet-and-greet. Teammates can comment on the application.
  3. Approve. An approved application puts the animal on Adoption pending, and other applications for that animal are waitlisted.
  4. Finalize. The adopter signs the contract on a tablet or by email, pays the fee, and the animal's status changes to Adopted.
Important:
Adopter and foster records contain personal information. Share them only with staff who need them, and never export them to personal devices.

Fosters

Foster placements use the same workflow, with a foster agreement in place of an adoption contract. A foster animal stays available for adoption unless you turn off List while in foster.
