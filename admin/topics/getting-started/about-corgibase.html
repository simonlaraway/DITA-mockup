About CorgiBase

CorgiBase is a cloud-based shelter management platform from CorgiBase, Inc.. It tracks every animal from intake to adoption, so your team spends less time on paperwork and more time with the dogs.

Shelters of every size use CorgiBase, from foster-based rescues with a handful of volunteers to municipal shelters with hundreds of kennels. Everything runs in the browser at app.corgibase.example. You don't install or update anything, and new features arrive automatically. This guide covers release 2026.10.

What you can do

  • Track animals. Keep one record per animal, with photos, kennel location, behavior notes and a full history.
  • Manage medical care. Log vaccinations and treatments, and see what's due today across the whole shelter.
  • Run adoptions. Take applications online, review them as a team, and finalize adoptions with digital contracts.
  • Coordinate volunteers. Publish shifts, let volunteers sign up, and track hours.
  • Connect other systems. Use the CorgiBase REST API to sync adoptable animals to your website or pull data into your own reports. See the CorgiBase API Guide.

Web and mobile

Staff and volunteers can also use the CorgiBase Kennel app for iOS and Android. It's designed for the kennel floor: scan an animal's collar tag, take photos, and log feedings or medication without going back to a desk. The app works offline and syncs when it reconnects.
