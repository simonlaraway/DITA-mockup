Logging a vaccination

Record a vaccination so the animal's care plan and certificate stay up to date.

Before you begin

You need the Vet staff role, or the Staff role for vaccines covered by a standing protocol.

Procedure

  1. Open the animal's record: press /, type the animal's name or ID, and select it from the results.
  2. On the Medical tab, find the due vaccination in the care plan and click Record.

    For a vaccination that isn't in the care plan, click Add > Vaccination instead.

  3. Scan the vial's barcode, or enter the product and lot number by hand.
  4. Confirm the date, the route and who gave it.
  5. Click Save.

Results

The vaccination is marked as given, and any booster is scheduled automatically. The vaccination certificate on the Documents tab updates to include it.
