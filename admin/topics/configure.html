Configuring Acme Widget

Adjust preferences to suit your workflow.

Before you begin

Tip:
Export your configuration before you upgrade so you can roll back if needed.

Procedure

  1. Open Settings > General.
  2. Choose a theme and notification level.
  3. [Admin]Enable audit logging under Settings > Security.
  4. [Admin]Push the configuration to all enrolled devices.
  5. Click Save.

What to do next

For a full list of options, see Settings reference.
