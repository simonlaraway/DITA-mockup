Inviting and removing users

Give staff and volunteers access to CorgiBase, change their roles, and remove access when they leave.

Before you begin

Note:
Only users with the Shelter administrator or Owner role can do this.

Procedure

  1. Open Settings > Users.
  2. Click Invite users and enter one or more email addresses.

    To invite many volunteers at once, paste a list of addresses, one per line.

  3. Choose a role for the new users, and optionally limit them to one site.
  4. Click Send invitations.

Results

Invited users appear with status Pending until they accept.

What to do next

To remove a user, open their profile and click Deactivate. They're signed out everywhere immediately. Their name stays on the records they created, so your history remains intact. Deactivated users don't count toward your plan's user limit.
