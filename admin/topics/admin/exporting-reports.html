Exporting reports

Download intake, outcome and medical data for board reports, grant applications and industry reporting such as Shelter Animals Count.

Before you begin

Note:
Only users with the Shelter administrator or Owner role can do this.

Procedure

  1. Click Reports.
  2. Choose a report.
    ReportContains
    Intake and outcomeCounts by species, type and outcome. Matches the Shelter Animals Count format.
    Length of stayAverage and median days from intake to outcome.
    Medical activityVaccinations, treatments and surgeries by date.
    Volunteer hoursHours by volunteer and shift type.
  3. Set the date range and, if you have several sites, the site.
  4. Click Export and choose CSV or PDF.

Results

Small reports download immediately. Larger ones are emailed to you as a link that expires after 7 days.

What to do next

Important:
Adopter and foster records contain personal information. Share them only with staff who need them, and never export them to personal devices.
