Plans and billing

CorgiBase is billed per organization, monthly or yearly. Nonprofit shelters get 30% off any plan.

Changing your plan

Only the account Owner can change plans or payment details, in Settings > Billing. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period.
