Assigning an animal to a kennel

Move an animal into a kennel, or between kennels, so everyone knows where to find them.

Procedure

  1. Open the animal's record: press /, type the animal's name or ID, and select it from the results.
  2. Click Move.
  3. Select a kennel on the map. Available kennels are green; kennels with a compatibility warning are amber.

    Hover over an amber kennel to see the warning, for example "Neighbor is reactive to large dogs".

  4. Click Confirm move.

Results

The move is added to the animal's timeline, and the kennel card is ready to print from Print card.

What to do next

To house two animals together, move the second animal into the same kennel and select Co-house when prompted.
