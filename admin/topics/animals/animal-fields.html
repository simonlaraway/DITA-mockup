Animal record fields

Fields on the Profile tab of an animal record, and where each one appears.

FieldFormatNotes
Animal IDCB-YYYY-NNNNNAssigned at intake. Read-only.
NameText, 60 charactersShown on kennel cards and listings.
SpeciesDog, Cat, Rabbit, OtherControls which vaccination schedule applies.
BreedPrimary and secondaryChoose from the list, or select Mixed.
Estimated birth dateDateAge is calculated from this. Use the 1st of the month if unsure.
SizeXS, S, M, L, XLBased on adult weight.
Microchip9, 10 or 15 digitsChecked against the registry when saved.
AlteredYes, No, UnknownSpayed or neutered. Required before adoption in most regions.
Public descriptionRich textPublished on adoption listings. Write it for adopters.
[Admin]Internal cost code[Admin]Text[Admin]Used in cost reports. Defaults from shelter settings.
