Uploading animal photos

Good photos are the biggest factor in how quickly an animal is adopted. Add them from a computer or straight from your phone.

About this task

Each animal can have up to 20 photos and 3 videos. The first photo is the primary photo on listings and kennel cards.

Procedure

  1. Open the animal's record: press /, type the animal's name or ID, and select it from the results.
  2. On the Profile tab, drag photos onto the photo strip, or click Add photos.
  3. In the CorgiBase Kennel app, tap Camera and take the photo. It uploads automatically, even if you're offline when you take it.
  4. Drag the best photo to the first position to make it the primary photo.
  5. Optional: click a photo and add a caption.

    Captions are read aloud by screen readers on your public listings, so describe what's in the photo.

Results

New photos appear on public listings within 15 minutes.
