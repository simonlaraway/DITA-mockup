Recording an animal intake

Create a record for an animal that's arriving at your shelter, whether it's a stray, an owner surrender or a transfer.

Before you begin

Scan the animal for a microchip before you start. CorgiBase checks the number against your records and the national registry.

Procedure

  1. Sign in to CorgiBase at app.corgibase.example.
  2. Click Animals > New intake.
  3. Choose the intake type.
    TypeUse when
    StrayThe animal was found. Record where and by whom. A stray hold starts automatically.
    Owner surrenderAn owner is giving up the animal. Collect their contact details and reason for surrender.
    Transfer inThe animal comes from a partner organization. Select the partner from the list.
    ReturnA previously adopted animal is coming back. Search for the existing record instead of creating a new one.
  4. Enter the microchip number, or select No chip found.

    If the chip matches an existing record, CorgiBase offers to open it.

  5. Fill in the animal's name, species, breed, sex, estimated age and weight.

    If the animal has no name, leave the field blank; CorgiBase assigns a temporary one, like "Intake 0412", that you can change later.

  6. Click Create record.

Results

The new record opens with status Intake and a permanent animal ID. Next, assign the animal to a kennel.
