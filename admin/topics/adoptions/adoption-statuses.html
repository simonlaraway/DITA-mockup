Animal statuses

The statuses an animal can have. Statuses change automatically when you record events.

StatusListed?Set when
IntakeNoA new record is created.
Stray holdNoA stray intake is recorded. Ends after your region's hold period.
Medical holdNoA vet places a hold.
AvailableYesAll holds have ended and the animal is marked ready.
In fosterOptionalA foster placement is finalized.
Adoption pendingMarked "pending"An application is approved.
AdoptedNoAn adoption is finalized.
Transferred outNoThe animal moves to a partner organization.
Returned to ownerNoA stray is reclaimed by its owner.
