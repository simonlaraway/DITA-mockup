Processing an adoption application

Review an application, approve or decline it, and finalize the adoption.

Before you begin

Important:
Adopter and foster records contain personal information. Share them only with staff who need them, and never export them to personal devices.

Procedure

  1. Click Adoptions and open an application with status New.
  2. Click Start review so teammates know you're working on it.
  3. Work through the review checklist.
    1. Call at least one reference and record the outcome.
    2. If the adopter rents, confirm landlord approval.
    3. Check the adopter's history for previous returns or do-not-adopt flags.
  4. Choose an outcome.
    • Approve: the animal moves to Adoption pending.
    • Decline: choose a reason. The adopter receives your shelter's decline email.
  5. When the adopter arrives, click Finalize, collect the signature and fee, and print the go-home packet.

Results

The animal's status changes to Adopted, its listing is removed, and a follow-up survey is scheduled for 2 weeks later.
