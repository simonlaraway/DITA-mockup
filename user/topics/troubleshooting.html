Troubleshooting

Fix common problems with Acme Widget.

The widget does not start, or the status light blinks red.

A hardware fault or a corrupted configuration.

Procedure

  1. Restart the Acme Widget service.
  2. Check the cable connections.
    Warning:
    Disconnect the Acme Widget from power before you open the housing.

The problem is not listed here.

If the problem persists, contact Acme Support Portal.
