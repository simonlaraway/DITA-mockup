Roles and permissions

Every user has one role. The role decides what that user can see and change.

PermissionVolunteerStaff Vet staffShelter administratorOwner
View animals and photosYesYesYesYesYes
Log feedings and walksYesYesYesYesYes
Record intakes and movesNoYesYesYesYes
Log vaccinations and treatmentsNoLimitedYesYesYes
Review and approve adoptionsNoYesNoYesYes
View adopter contact detailsNoYesNoYesYes

Notes

"Limited" means staff can record vaccinations given under a standing protocol but can't record diagnoses or prescriptions. Ask an administrator if you need a different role.
