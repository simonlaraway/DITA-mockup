How CorgiBase organizes shelter data

Everything in CorgiBase connects to a small set of records: animals, people, locations and events. Knowing how they relate makes the rest of the app easier to navigate.

Animal
The central record. Each animal gets a permanent ID such as CB-2026-00412 at intake, and that ID never changes, even if the animal is adopted and later returned.
Person
Anyone outside your team who interacts with an animal: adopters, fosters, finders and surrendering owners. One person can have many roles over time.
Location
Where an animal is right now: a kennel, a foster home, a partner clinic or an offsite adoption event. Locations belong to a site if your organization runs more than one facility.
Event
Anything that happens to an animal: intake, a vaccination, a move, an application, an adoption. Events are append-only, so every record has a complete, auditable timeline.

Status is derived from events

You never edit an animal's status directly. Recording an event, such as approving an adoption, moves the animal to the right status automatically. This keeps reports accurate and makes every status change traceable to a person and a time.
