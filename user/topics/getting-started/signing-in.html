Signing in for the first time

Accept your invitation, set a password, and turn on two-step verification.

Before you begin

An administrator at your shelter must invite you first. Look for an email from CorgiBase with the subject "You're invited to join your shelter".

Procedure

  1. Open the invitation email and click Accept invitation.

    Invitations expire after 7 days. If yours has expired, ask your administrator to resend it.

  2. Enter your name and choose a password of at least 12 characters.
  3. Set up two-step verification.
    • Scan the QR code with an authenticator app, then enter the 6-digit code.
    • Or register a security key or passkey.

    You see 10 backup codes. Store them somewhere safe; each one works once if you lose your device.

  4. Click Finish.

Results

The Today dashboard opens. It shows the animals, tasks and shifts that need your attention.

What to do next

Install the CorgiBase Kennel app on your phone and sign in with the same account to work from the kennel floor.
