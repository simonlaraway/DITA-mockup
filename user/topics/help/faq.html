Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions shelters ask most about CorgiBase.

Is our data safe?

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, backed up every hour, and stored in the region you chose at sign-up. CorgiBase, Inc. never sells or shares shelter data.

Can we import records from our old system?

Yes. During onboarding, our team imports animals, people and medical history from a CSV export or directly from most common shelter systems, at no charge.

Does it work without internet?

The web app needs a connection. The CorgiBase Kennel app works offline for everyday kennel tasks and syncs when it reconnects.

Why can't I see adopter phone numbers?

Your role doesn't include access to adopter contact details. See Roles and permissions, or ask your administrator.

Something isn't working. What should I do?

Try signing out and back in. If the problem continues, note what you were doing and when, then contact support.

If you still need help, contact CorgiBase Support or email support@corgibase.example. Check status.corgibase.example for service incidents.
