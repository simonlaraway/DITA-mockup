Scheduling volunteer shifts

Publish shifts that volunteers can sign up for, and track their hours.

Procedure

  1. Click Volunteers > Shifts.
  2. Click New shift and enter the date, time and number of volunteers needed.
  3. Choose a shift type, such as Dog walking, Kennel cleaning or Adoption event.

    Shift types can require training. Volunteers only see shifts they're trained for.

  4. Optional: select Repeat weekly.
  5. Click Publish.

Results

Trained volunteers get a notification and can sign up in the CorgiBase Kennel app. Volunteers check in and out with the app, and their hours are recorded automatically.
