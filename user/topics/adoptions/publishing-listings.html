Publishing adoptable animals to your website

Show your available animals on your own website, and let adopters apply online.

About this task

CorgiBase hosts a listing page for your shelter automatically. You can link to it, embed it in your website, or build your own page with the CorgiBase API Guide.

Procedure

  1. Click Get embed code and copy the snippet.
  2. Paste the snippet into your website where the listings should appear. 
    <div id="corgibase-listings" data-shelter="your-shelter"></div>
<script src="https://embed.corgibase.example/listings.js" async></script>

Results

Your listings update automatically when an animal's status, photos or description change.
