Medical records and care plans

CorgiBase keeps a medical history for every animal and builds a care plan from your shelter's protocols, so nothing is missed.

When an animal is taken in, CorgiBase applies your shelter's intake protocol and creates a care plan: a list of vaccinations, treatments and checks with due dates. Staff see what's due on the Today dashboard and in the CorgiBase Kennel app.

What a medical record contains

  • Vaccinations, with product, lot number and who gave them
  • Treatments and prescriptions
  • Exams and test results, such as heartworm and FIV/FeLV
  • Weight history, charted over time
  • Surgeries, including spay and neuter

Medical holds

A vet can place an animal on Medical hold. While the hold is in place, the animal can't be listed for adoption or moved to a foster home. The hold ends when a vet clears it.
