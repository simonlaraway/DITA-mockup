Default dog vaccination schedule

The schedule CorgiBase uses to build care plans for dogs, unless your shelter has customized it.

VaccineFirst doseBoosters
DHPPAt intake (from 6 weeks)Every 2 weeks until 20 weeks old, then yearly
BordetellaAt intakeYearly
RabiesAt 12 weeks or at intake if olderAfter 1 year, then every 3 years
Canine influenza (H3N2/H3N8)At intake, if enabledAfter 2–4 weeks, then yearly
LeptospirosisFrom 12 weeks, if enabledAfter 2–4 weeks, then yearly

Customizing the schedule

Schedules vary by region and veterinary guidance. Administrators can change this schedule in Settings > Medical protocols.
